Share a local port

Make a service reachable for friends — on your machine, or anywhere on your network. They connect with steam-tunnel using this port number. Leave forward to empty for localhost:<port>, or point it at another device, e.g. 192.168.1.50:8096 to share your NAS's Jellyfin.
Pick a friend, then press invite on a shared port — they get a Steam invite and, if their steam-tunnel is running, an accept prompt appears in their web UI.

Connect to a friend's port

Friend: who is sharing the port (type to search).
Their port: the port number your friend shared on their machine — ask them, or accept their invite.
Local port: where it shows up on your machine; point your program at localhost:<local port>. Leave empty to use the same number.
